AI Agents

Not selling agents. Getting work done.

Repetitive tasks eat up focus. That is exactly what my AI agents take over: they build and check API calls, write quotes and invoices, research and document. Not a buzzword topic, but real work that simply disappears from the daily routine, so people can focus on what matters.

Case Study

Wolfgang, the first employee.

Wolfgang is an AI agent I trained on my knowledge of SAP OMF and OMSA. He lives in a Telegram chat and takes over exactly the tasks that would otherwise cost focus: he builds the right API payloads, executes the calls, analyses the responses and documents the result.

When testing and configuring in an SAP environment, small slips – a wrong field, a wrong material, a wrong work centre – quickly send you debugging in completely the wrong direction. Wolfgang takes over exactly this error-prone manual work, around the clock and with endless patience.

The real lever: specialist knowledge is no longer only in my head. Colleagues simply ask Wolfgang instead of coming back to me again and again, and get a reliable answer right away.
